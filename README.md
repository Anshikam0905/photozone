# 📸 Photozone -- Indian Photo & Portrait Studio

A modern and elegant **Photography Studio website** designed for an
Indian audience.\
The website presents professional photography services, creative
portfolios, photographers, customer testimonials, and studio information
in a clean and premium layout.

## 🌟 Project Overview

**Photozone** is a responsive photography studio website concept created
for an Indian photography business. It focuses on showcasing memorable
moments such as weddings, portraits, fashion, travel, and editorial
photography.

The design uses a warm, minimal visual style with image-focused sections
to give visitors a premium photography experience.

## 🎯 Main Sections

### 1. Hero Section

Introduces Photozone with the headline:

> **Wedding And Portrait Studio Based in India**

It includes a short description, an Explore More button, and a Play
Video call-to-action.

### 2. About Us

Explains the studio's professional photography approach and highlights
its experience in Indian wedding, portrait, and lifestyle photography.

### 3. Studio Achievements

Displays important statistics such as:

-   **35+** Award Winning
-   **15+** Years Experience
-   **12500+** Happy Clients

These values can be customized according to the real business.

### 4. Photography Services

The website highlights professional services including:

-   Weddings
-   Portraits
-   Fashion
-   Editorial

### 5. Creative Portfolio

A masonry-style gallery is used to showcase different photography
categories, including:

-   Memory
-   Travel
-   Wedding
-   Fashion
-   Portrait

### 6. Our Photographers

The team section introduces professional photographers with their
profile images, roles, short descriptions, and social links.

### 7. Customer Testimonials

A testimonial section builds trust by displaying feedback from previous
customers.

### 8. Footer

The footer contains:

-   Studio information
-   Quick links
-   Popular links
-   Newsletter subscription
-   Contact details
-   Social media links
